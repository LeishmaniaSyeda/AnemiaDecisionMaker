02 / The mental model

Three patterns. Different questions.

MCV narrows the field. The reticulocyte response tells you whether the marrow is keeping up.

↓

Microcytic

MCV <80 fL

Question: Can the marrow make haemoglobin?

Think impaired haemoglobin synthesis. Iron deficiency is common, but thalassaemia trait and inflammation can look similar.

  • Ferritin ± CRP, iron profile / TSAT
  • RBC count, RDW and blood film
  • Hb electrophoresis / HPLC if indicated
↔

Normocytic

MCV 80–100 fL

Question: Is blood being lost / destroyed, or not produced?

Retics split the pathway: a strong response suggests loss or destruction; a weak response suggests underproduction.

  • High retics: bleeding or haemolysis
  • Low retics: renal, inflammatory, endocrine or marrow causes
  • Check other cell lines and the smear
↑

Macrocytic

MCV >100 fL

Question: Is DNA synthesis impaired, or are cells simply larger?

B12 and folate deficiency cause megaloblastosis. Alcohol, liver disease, hypothyroidism, drugs and reticulocytosis can also raise MCV.

  • Blood film + reticulocyte count
  • B12 and folate; consider MMA if uncertain
  • Review alcohol, liver, thyroid and medicines

Start every work-up with

  • Confirm anemia: in adults, Hb <13 g/dL in men or <12 g/dL in non-pregnant women (use age-, sex- and pregnancy-specific ranges where relevant)
  • CBC including WBC, platelets, RBC count, MCV and RDW
  • Reticulocyte count (absolute count or corrected index)
  • Peripheral blood smear
  • History and examination directed at bleeding, diet, drugs, chronic disease, family history and haemolysis

Stop the routine algorithm if you see these

  • Haemodynamic instability, active major bleeding, syncope, chest pain or breathlessness at rest
  • Rapid Hb fall or suspected acute haemolysis
  • Schistocytes with thrombocytopenia, or suspected TTP / DIC
  • Pancytopenia, blasts, severe neutropenia or a rapidly evolving blood film
  • New neurological signs with suspected B12 deficiency
Urgent clinical assessment / emergency care is needed. This learning tool is not a triage service.
