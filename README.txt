ANEMIA / NEXT STEP

A self-contained educational website about adult anemia evaluation and treatment principles.

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP (if you received the ZIP).
2. Open index.html in any modern browser.
3. No installation, server, account or internet connection is needed for the interactive features. External guideline links require internet access.

SCOPE & SAFETY
- Designed as an educational aid for adult anemia reasoning.
- Not a clinical decision support system or a substitute for clinician assessment.
- Pediatric anemia, pregnancy, transfusion decisions and complex hematologic disorders require dedicated pathways and clinician oversight.
- Review linked guidelines and local protocols before clinical use.

CONTENTS
- Interactive next-step navigator
- MCV / reticulocyte approach
- Searchable treatment library
- Red flags
- Three short clinical reasoning cases
- Links to referenced guidelines and sources
